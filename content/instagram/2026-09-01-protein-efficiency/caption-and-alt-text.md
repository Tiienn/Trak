Want more protein without pushing calories up too quickly? Start with protein-efficient options: foods that deliver more protein for the calories they contain.

Shrimp, tuna, white fish, lean poultry, pork tenderloin, egg whites, whey isolate and 0% Greek yogurt can all make that easier.

The numbers are estimates—not rules. Brand, cut, cooking method, added oil and water loss can change them. And “more efficient” does not automatically mean “healthier.” The best protein source is still one that works for your preferences, budget and overall diet.
