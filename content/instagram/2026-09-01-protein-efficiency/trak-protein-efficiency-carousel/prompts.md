# Image-generation prompt

Built-in image generation was used for the cover photography background.

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: 4:5 Instagram carousel food photography background. Premium editorial overhead flat-lay of cooked shrimp, whey protein powder, tuna, egg whites, white fish, turkey breast, pork tenderloin, chicken breast and plain nonfat Greek yogurt on warm cream stone, with forest-green accents and generous central negative space. Photorealistic natural food photography, soft daylight, no people, labels, words, numbers, packaging, logos or watermark.

The typography and information design were composed deterministically after generation so all nutrition copy remains exact and legible.
