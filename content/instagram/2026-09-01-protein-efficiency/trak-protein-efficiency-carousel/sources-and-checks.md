# Sources and checks

- USDA FoodData Central: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/ — food-composition reference and basis for treating values as preparation-dependent estimates.
- USDA Foundation Foods documentation: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/Foundation_Foods_Documentation/ — explains that energy reflects protein, fat, carbohydrate and water-related composition, and documents Atwater factors.
- FDA Food Labeling Guide: https://www.fda.gov/media/81606/download — 9 kcal/g fat; 4 kcal/g protein and carbohydrate.
- Dymatize ISO100 product information: https://dymatize.com/iso100 — example whey isolate lists 25 g protein at up to 120 calories, supporting a range rather than the reference post’s single 106-calorie figure.

## Editorial improvements from the reference

- Reframed “calorie efficient” as a clear comparison: calories typically needed to obtain 25 g protein.
- Kept all nine source foods but split them across readable slides.
- Replaced false precision with approximate values and ranges where brand variation is substantial.
- Corrected the claim that fat is the sole driver; cooking, water, carbohydrate, cut, brand and additions also matter.
- Added context that protein efficiency is a tool, not a health ranking.

## Accuracy note

Values are rounded editorial estimates for plain foods and are not intended to replace a specific package label or weighed food entry. Preparation method, brand and database entry can materially change results.
