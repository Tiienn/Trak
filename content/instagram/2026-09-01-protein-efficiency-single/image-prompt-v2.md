# Food photography prompt

Built-in image generation was used to create a strict 3 × 3 source sheet of overhead food photographs in this order: shrimp, whey isolate, tuna, egg whites, white fish, turkey breast, pork tenderloin, chicken breast and 0% Greek yogurt. Each food was isolated on the same warm cream background with soft daylight, consistent scale and no text, packaging, people, utensils, logos or watermark.

The final post was composed separately so the names and nutrition values remain exact and legible.
