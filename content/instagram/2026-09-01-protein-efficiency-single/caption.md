If you’re trying to increase your protein without adding too many extra calories, leaner protein sources can make things easier.

The idea is simple: compare how many calories it takes to get roughly 25 g of protein. Shrimp, tuna, white fish, chicken breast, turkey breast and egg whites can provide plenty of protein for relatively few calories.

Leaner options often come out lower in this comparison because fat provides more calories per gram than protein. But the exact numbers still depend on the brand, cut, cooking method, sauces and added oil.

These are estimates—not food rules. You can include any protein source you enjoy. This list is simply useful when you want to reach your protein target while keeping calories more controlled.
