# Accuracy notes

- The 1.6 g/kg/day figure is a meta-regression point estimate where additional protein did not further contribute to resistance-training-induced gains in fat-free mass.
- It is approximately 0.73 g/lb/day, rounded to 0.7 for readability.
- It is not a universal upper limit. The confidence interval around the breakpoint was broad, and needs can differ during energy restriction, with age, health status and training context.
- One gram per pound remains a reasonable intake for people who prefer it; the post challenges necessity, not safety or personal preference.
