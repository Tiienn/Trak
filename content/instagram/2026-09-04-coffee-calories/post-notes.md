# Coffee calorie comparison

- Format: Instagram portrait, 1080 × 1350 px (4:5)
- Reference concept: compare the accumulated calories of one medium coffee drink per day.
- Values shown use current Starbucks U.S. standard recipes for a 473 ml (16 fl oz) cup: brewed black coffee 5, cappuccino 140, caramel macchiato 250, and mocha Frappuccino 370 calories per cup.
- Weekly figures multiply the per-drink examples by seven.
- The design and caption explicitly state that recipes, sizes, milk, syrup and toppings change the result.
- The reference post's fixed “3,500 calories equals one pound of fat” framing was intentionally omitted because real weight change is more complex and individual.

## Final image-generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Premium top-down food photograph of exactly four medium coffee drinks arranged in one horizontal row on a warm cream stone surface: black coffee, cappuccino, caramel latte, and mocha frappé. Soft daylight, realistic shadows, no people, props, logos, text or watermark.

The photographic asset was generated with the built-in image-generation tool. All typography, figures and Trak branding were added deterministically in the final layout.
