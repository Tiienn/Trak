Coffee itself is not automatically high in calories. What changes the picture is what goes into the cup.

Plain black coffee contains very few calories. Milk, flavored syrups, whipped cream and toppings can move a drink closer to the calorie range of a snack—or even a dessert.

That does not make sweet coffee “bad.” It simply means it is worth counting the drink you actually order, especially if it is part of your daily routine.

For a consistent comparison, these figures use current Starbucks U.S. standard recipes for a 473 ml (16 fl oz) cup. Calories can vary by country, cup size, milk choice and customization.
