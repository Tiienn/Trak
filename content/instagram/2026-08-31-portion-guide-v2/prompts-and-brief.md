# Portion Guide — corrected educational carousel

## Editorial rule

Reference supplies the educational content; Trak supplies visual branding only. No app explanations, product-benefit slides, install pitches or fabricated customer results. Original wording and visuals; preserve useful facts and examples, improve clarity and accuracy. This replaces the prior product-led creative direction; prior files are retained as history.

## Generation

Built-in image generation, one slide per call. Illustrative food visuals. Brand from existing Trak cream/green editorial design tokens. New outputs saved separately in v2.

## Shared prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Make ONE finished educational Instagram carousel slide, canvas 1122x1402 pixels, portrait 4:5. NOT a mockup or montage. Trak branding only, absolutely no app descriptions, download CTAs, phone UI, product benefits, nutrition tracker references or AI Body Analysis. Premium food editorial art direction: warm cream #F5F2E9, forest green #3D6B4F, dark warm ink #2B2721, subtle sage #E3EAD7; Georgia-like bold serif titles and large calorie numerals, clean highly readable sans-serif secondary text. 72px outer safe margin. Subtle paper texture, soft natural shadows, top-down appetizing photographic-style food illustrations. No recreation of competitor's design. Top-left small serif "Trak"; top-right small "THE PORTION GUIDE"; bottom-left "@trakwithyou"; bottom-right supplied slide number with thin right arrow except final slide. Neat consistent editorial grid, strong information hierarchy and very readable mobile text. Avoid fear, shaming, right/wrong portions, red crosses, diet rules, unsupported average portions. Each food comparison has equally treated left and right columns, actual food quantity visibly different (right exactly double left by amount, not enlarged individual food). Exact supplied text only. Visuals are illustrative, not claimed to be actual weighed food photographs. Do not add calorie values other than supplied. Footnotes are small but legible.
