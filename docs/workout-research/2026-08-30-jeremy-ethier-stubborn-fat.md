# Workout research: “How to Lose Every Type Of Stubborn Fat”

Status: approved for a limited catalogue/recommendation addition on 2026-09-01.

## Approved material

- Overhead cable and dumbbell triceps extensions at 6:51.
- Incline dumbbell press at 7:15.
- Machine chest fly at 7:25, without adopting the unsupported “forward lean targets upper chest” claim.
- Lateral raise at 9:29.
- Pull-up and an assisted progression at 9:31.
- Moderate steady cardio and carefully gated vigorous intervals at 2:40–3:13.
- Baseline-relative step progression as coaching context, not a fixed 10,000-step rule.
- A conservative rate-of-loss idea may inform future guardrails for lean, resistance-trained users, but 0.7% per week is not encoded as a universal maximum.

Strength prescriptions in the catalogue are Trak defaults, not prescriptions stated in the video. The video supplied no complete routine and no strength sets, reps, rest, or loading plan.

## Rejected material

- “Stubbornness” scores and fixed body-fat thresholds.
- Diagnosing visceral fat from appearance or abdominal firmness.
- Sex or ethnicity rules inferred from private DXA examples.
- Body weight in pounds multiplied by 10–13 as a calorie target.
- Universal under-2,000-calorie or 10,000-step instructions.
- Promises about face puffiness resolving within 24 hours.
- Any claim that an exercise burns fat from a named body area.
- Calorie-burn targets for cardio recommendations.
