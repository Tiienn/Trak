## Extracted routine

The video presents six foundation movements plus one or two optional accessories,
performed as a full-body session two or three times per week.

- 0:41: low-incline dumbbell press.
- 2:19: goblet squat, barbell squat, and heel-elevated or box setup variations.
- 4:23: inverted-row, assisted/neutral-grip pull-up, pull-up, and weighted progression.
- 5:49: dumbbell and barbell Romanian deadlift.
- 7:37: cable row.
- 8:55: supported lateral raise and prone incline rear-delt raise.
- 10:22: dead bug, hip abduction, calf raise, incline dumbbell curl with overhead
  triceps extension, incline Kelso shrug, and chest fly accessories.
- 12:17: beginner, glute-emphasis, and advanced routine summaries.

The listed workouts use three sets. Rep ranges vary by exercise and experience:
presses 6–15, squats 6–15, pull-up variations 5–15, Romanian deadlifts 6–15,
cable rows 10–15, and shoulder raises 10–20. The video does not prescribe rest
intervals or cardio duration, intensity, or calories.

## Approved recommendation behavior

Catalogue exercises may carry one foundation movement pattern: press, knee-dominant,
vertical pull, hip hinge, horizontal pull, or shoulder accessory. A requested
full-body session selects distinct patterns according to available time:

- under 25 minutes: up to two patterns;
- 25–39 minutes: up to three;
- 40–49 minutes: up to four;
- 50–59 minutes: up to five;
- 60 minutes or more: up to six.

Recent exercise history modestly favours patterns omitted from the most recent
recommendations. Goal, experience, equipment, location, limitations, seven-day muscle
sets, attention areas, load history, and the total time budget still apply. Optional
accessories can be added only after foundation patterns and only if the caller allows
more recommendation slots and time remains.

Recommendations do not earn points. Only marking training complete records Workout
Time and completed sets for Weekly Muscle Score.

## Rejected material

- “Only workout needed” and universal best-results claims.
- Sex-specific pull-up labels such as fit, advanced, or elite.
- A half-bodyweight goblet squat as a mandatory barbell-squat gateway.
- Diagnosing a perfect press angle from a phone placed on the sternum.
- Treating squat stance as determinable from appearance rather than comfort and control.
- Promising that heel elevation or a box isolates a named muscle.
- More knee bend during a Romanian deadlift as a guaranteed glute-targeting method.
- Mandatory failure, post-failure scapular burnouts, or ego-driven loading.
- Claims that dead bugs instantly improve every compound lift.
- Appearance promises such as creating an “upper-glute shelf.”
- Leaning forward in a fly as a proven method of isolating upper-chest fibres.
- Automatically assigning all six foundation exercises when time or recent training
  does not support them.
